Our Story

Made by hand, meant to be remembered

La Crème began in a home kitchen and grew on word of mouth alone, one wedding cake, one catering spread, one satisfied host at a time. Today, every order still passes through the same hands, the same recipes, and the same insistence that nothing leaves the kitchen unless it's right.

We cater indoor and outdoor events of every size, from an intimate birthday table to a full wedding reception, and we build every menu around what you're actually celebrating.
